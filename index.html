How a lab works

Not a lesson with a quiz on the end. A bench with things on it that a child moves, and a result that answers back.

1

Do the thing

Split a week's drops between three gates. Price your own stall. Buy a slice of a bakery. The decision is made with your hands, not by picking A, B or C.

2

Live with it

Then the week runs, or the seasons pass, and your choice arrives back as an outcome. Nobody marks it right or wrong — it simply happens, the way money does.

3

Learn what it's called

Only now does the real word appear: opportunity cost, compounding, volatility. A name for something already felt sticks; a definition learned first does not.

Why it helps

💧

Everything is drops

One honest unit all the way through, so no lab is really about rupees or dollars. A child who has moved drops can move any currency later.

🧪

A workbench, not a quiz

Nothing is ever scored. There's no streak to protect and no wrong answer to be caught by — which is exactly what makes it safe to try the reckless option and watch what it costs.

🌱

The values are built in, never preached

Sharing is a real gate that moves real drops, not a sticker for being nice. Helping at home earns nothing — because it's your home — and the app says so plainly.

🎯

Honest about the hard parts

A business can fail and take your drops with it. Borrowing costs more the slower you repay. A price falling tells you nothing on its own. None of it is softened.

🏡

Screen time, your call

Optional parent controls: a profile for each child, a daily limit from 15 minutes to 2 hours behind your PIN, and a list of recent sessions.

✈️

Private & offline

Ad-free, no tracking, and every lab is stored on the device so it all works with no connection. Nothing a child does in a lab leaves their browser.

The ladder

Five chapters and 25 labs, from why money had to be invented to the number a stranger writes down about you. Every lab here is built and playable — and there are no locks, so a child starts wherever it looks interesting.

Inside a lab

Take Earn & the Three Gates — what a child actually does, and why it's shaped that way.

🚪

Three gates, one week's drops

Everything earned has to go somewhere: spending, saving, or sharing. Move the drops yourself. There's no recommended split and no gate you're told to favour.

📅

Then live the week

The week runs. Something you wanted comes up, and either the spending gate covers it or it doesn't. That's the whole lesson, and nobody has to say it out loud.

🧹

Chores pay nothing

Helping at home sits on the same board as the paid jobs and is worth no drops — on purpose. It's your home; you help because you live in it. The one place the app takes a position.
